### Title: The Fires of Mt. Morris

#### Main Premise - Lots of Fires
During my research on another topic I discovered some interesting history of the town of Mt. Morris, Illions.

#### Rock River Seminary
Established in 1837 the town of Mt Morris would quickly establish themselves as a place of higher learning. Constructing the First Higher Education Institution of Rock River Seminary by the Methodist Church in 1839. 
It would operate until 1878 when due to financial struggles it would be forced to close it's doors. Some noteable alumni of Rock River Seminary include:
    - Smith D. Atkins, Civil War General
    - John L. Beveridge, Governor of Illinois
    - Shelby M. Cullom, Congressman, Senator, Governor of Illinois
    - Robert R. Hitt, Congressman
    - Daniel H. Johnson, Wisconsin Assemblyman
    - W.H.L Wallace, Civil War General


#### Mt. Morris College
After the closure of Rock River Seminary it was quickly purchased by the Church of the Brethren, reorganized and reopened in 1879 as Mount Morris Seminary and Collegiate Institute, and renamed to Mount Morris College in 1884. 
The college initially attracted many people to it's halls but in January of 1912 one of the college's main buildings would be burnt to the ground. This will be noted as Fire #1
After this fire the College would start a fundrasier to help rebuild the building which would be successful and a new building would be constructed.

However after the end of WW1 the enrollments would begin to decline as soldiers returned home looking for work rather than education and financial problems would begin to cause the school to struggle. (where have we heard this before). Enrollments would start to increase again in 1929 to 1930 but another Fire in April of 1932 would destroy a dormitory and heavily damage some classrooms and admin buildings on the campus. This will be noted as Fire #2. 

After this second fire with the already mounting financial struggles the college was already facing and with it happening at the height of the Great Depression, the college would be forced to close at the end of the school year in May of 1932.

#### Local Schools
Skipping forward to 1993 the village of Mt. Morris was renovating their old Jr. High School when it mysteriously caught fire. This then forced the merger of the town into the Oregon Illions School district. This will be noted as Fire #3

And in February of 2004, theh Rahn Elementary School would also be lost to a fire, resulting in the entire Oregon School District to be reorganized where now the Mt. Morris High School is used as the District's Middle School. This will be noted as Fire #4

#### Liberty Bell
The village of Mt. Morris sometime after 1926 found a small 16" diameter bell and mounted it on a cedar post behind the Veterans Memorial Fountain in the town square (which at the time was still the Mt. Morris College Campus). In 1963 this bell would be rung in conjunction with the Philadelphia Liberty Bell in a newly passed cerermony signifing the signing of the declartion of independence "the anniversary of the signing of the Declaration of Independence should be observed each year by the ringing of bells throughout the United States at the hour of 2 o’clock, eastern daylight time, in the afternoon of the fourth day of July".

This would begin the Tradition and designation of the Illinois Freedom Bell in Mt. Morris Illinois. There is more interesting history about the Illinois Freedom Bell that could be it's own topic, but for this video here is what I wanted to cover.

In 1966 with this tradition now firmly planted in the Mt. Morris community the Village wanted a Larger Bell. The committee was able to find one that was over 100 years old at the bottom of Lake Geneva, Wisconsin. 

This bell was originally cast as a replica of the Liberty Bell in 1862 and was located in a church on the north shore of Lake Geneva. However in 1910 the church **caught fire** and the bell fell from it's tower but was undamged. It was placed into storage and the church decided to rebuild on the south shore of Lake Geneva, during the winter a crew was transporting it across the ice which cracked underneath them and the bell plunged to the bottom of the lake.

It was resurfaced in 1960 by a wealthy resident of Lake Geneva where he decided he didn't need it and sold it to a farmer. After seeing the news of it's resurfacing the Mt. Morris committee bought the bell from the farmer for $500 in 1966 and placed it in a gazebo on the town square. In 1971 it was designated as the Offical Illinois Freedom Bell by Governor Richard Ogilvie.

#### Recap
So to recap, the Mt. Morris College suffered a Fire in 1912 and again in 1932 which forced it to close. A 20 year gap. 60 years later in 1993 another educational building burnt down forcing the residents to merge with a nearby school district, and 11 years later in 2004 a 4th fire would claim another educational facility forcing the complete merger with the nearby Oregon school district. and to top it all off, sitting no more 200ft away from the reconstructed Mt. Morris College Hall sits a bell that was lost under a lake due to a fire in 1910 at a church in Wisconsin.
