Have you ever asked yourself a question that took you down a rabbit hole so deep you had to start a Youtube channel to document it all? No? Just me?

Now how does a simple question 'Are there any notable people that grew up in my small rural town?' Lead to this, well lets start with my Hometown of Deer Creek Illinois. 

It's a small rural town pretty much smack dab between two larger cities in central Illinois Bloomington and Peoria, we have a population of around 700. No grocery store and only 1 bar. Sounds pretty boring if you didn't grow up in a rural environment, you would be right. But the catalyst of all this started when my wife and I took our son to the local library for the weekly story time. I being not a toddler was bored out of my mind and started looking at all the books on the shelves and found one called 'The History of Deer Creek'. I've always liked history so I picked it up and started skimming the pages. 

The Village of Deer Creek (originally called Mud Creek) was founded by Richard N Cullom, originally from Kentucky he and his 7 kids migrated to central Illinois and established the village along Mud Creek, hence the name.

One of his seven kids would be Shelby M Cullom, who is the main focus of this video. Shelby at a young age was focused on Education. He would attend the local school houses and after a time he would start teaching at those schoolhouses for 6 months out of the year at $20/month
